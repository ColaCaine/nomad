A steady loop for building web software: get clear on the problem, make a small change, prove it works, and ship with confidence.

THE DEVELOPMENT LOOP

↶ Learn and iterate
⌕ 01

Understand

Clarify the user, the problem, and what “done” should look like.

OUTCOME · Clear scope
◇ 02

Explore & plan

Inspect the codebase, identify constraints, and choose a small approach.

OUTCOME · Small plan
⌘ 03

Implement

Make the focused change, following existing patterns and keeping it reviewable.

OUTCOME · Working change
✓ 04

Verify

Run relevant tests, check edge cases, and review the result in context.

OUTCOME · Proven behavior
↗ 05

Review & ship

Share the change, document what matters, and monitor after release.

OUTCOME · Useful release
↶ Checks fail? Use what you learned to adjust the plan or implementation, then verify again.
